KM

I build dependable software because people deserve technology that gets out of their way.

I’m Kalidhasan Mariyappan, a senior full-stack developer and team lead from Tamil Nadu. Over six years, I’ve grown from hands-on engineering into leading delivery for complex enterprise applications — without losing the curiosity that made me start.

Healthcare taught me that details carry weight. A clear workflow can save time; secure architecture protects trust; thoughtful collaboration keeps ambitious work moving. That belief guides how I design systems, mentor developers, and partner with stakeholders.

Away from delivery plans and pull requests, I enjoy exploring AI-assisted development tools, learning new frameworks, and finding simpler ways to explain difficult technical ideas.
