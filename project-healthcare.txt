## PracticeManager — Project Explanation

### 1. What is PracticeManager?

**PracticeManager** is a multi-tenant healthcare application used by medical/dental practices to manage the complete lifecycle of a patient.

It covers everything from:

**Patient referral → Registration → Appointment → Clinical treatment → Insurance → Billing → Payment → Follow-up → Reporting**

So the application is not just storing patient records. It is running the **day-to-day operations of a healthcare practice**.

A simplified flow is:

```text
Patient / Referral
      ↓
Registration
      ↓
Insurance
      ↓
Appointment Scheduling
      ↓
Check-In
      ↓
Clinical Examination
      ↓
Treatment / Procedure
      ↓
Clinical Documentation
      ↓
Insurance Claim
      ↓
Invoice
      ↓
Payment / Collection
      ↓
Reports / Follow-up
```

# 41. The Business Purpose in One Story

Imagine a patient named **John** is referred to an oral-surgery practice.

The referring dentist sends John to the practice.

PracticeManager creates or identifies John's patient record.

John receives a registration link and completes his demographic, medical, and insurance information through the Patient Portal.

His insurance details are checked.

The front desk schedules an appointment with the correct surgeon.

John receives his confirmation and reminder.

On appointment day, John completes check-in and any required forms.

The surgeon opens John's EMR, reviews his history and imaging, performs an examination, creates a diagnosis and treatment plan, and eventually performs the procedure.

Clinical notes and procedure information become part of John's medical record.

The billing workflow calculates charges.

If insurance applies, PracticeManager generates the necessary claim data and sends it through the appropriate medical/dental EDI workflow.

The payer processes the claim.

Any remaining patient responsibility becomes an invoice.

John receives the invoice and pays through the PayROC integration.

The transaction is recorded.

If money remains outstanding, it appears in accounts receivable.

At the same time, management can use reporting modules to understand appointments, claims, payments, provider activity, and practice performance.

**That complete journey is essentially what your PracticeManager platform exists to manage.**
