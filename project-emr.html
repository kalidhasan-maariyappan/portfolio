The platform

PracticeManager is an end-to-end healthcare practice-management ecosystem, not only an EMR screen.

It supports the operating backbone of a medical, dental, or oral-surgery practice: patient referrals, registration, insurance, appointments, check-in, forms, clinical documentation, treatment planning, procedures, claims, billing, payments, accounts receivable, reporting, communication, and administration.

Core journey

The system follows the complete patient and revenue lifecycle from first contact to final payment.

01 - Referral & registrationPatients enter through staff workflows, Patient Portal links, or referral/collaborator workflows with demographics, insurance, medical history, responsible party, forms, and communication preferences.
02 - Scheduling & check-inProvider availability, FullCalendar scheduling, appointment type/subtype filters, confirmations, reminders, check-in links, forms completion, and front-desk readiness.
03 - Clinical careProviders manage EMR records, medical history, examination findings, diagnosis, treatment plans, procedures, anesthesia notes, clinical documentation, signatures, documents, and imaging.
04 - Insurance & EDIEligibility, medical and dental insurance workflows, X12 837P/837D generation, validation, parsing, ClaimMD, Vyne, clearinghouse submission, payer response, rejection, and payment updates.
05 - Billing & paymentsProcedure charges, patient responsibility, invoices, PayROC sales, keyed transactions, refunds, voids, receipts, recurring/batch behavior, transaction history, and A/R aging.
06 - Reporting & operationsPatient, appointment, provider, claims, financial, transaction, collections, and practice-performance reports built from connected clinical and financial data.
